Where onboarding resumes after the app closes

What happens when someone closes or loses the app at each onboarding step, what gets saved, and which screen they see when they open it again.

The rule

Resume at the first step whose outcome isn’t saved. Save every answer the moment it’s given: on the server, with a local copy for offline launches. For anything iOS controls (permissions), check the real state on launch instead of trusting a saved flag.

Nothing is saved on the server until OTP is verified, so closing before that starts again from the phone number.

Edge cases

Closed while in Phone or Settings

If iOS ends the app while someone is dialling the forwarding code or changing voicemail settings, treat it as “came back”. These are the most common closes, and nobody should redo the step.

Logged out or session expired

Back to Phone. The answers stay on the account, so after OTP they land on the first unanswered step.

Deleted and reinstalled

After OTP the server’s record wins. They resume at the same step and never see completed steps again.

Offline on launch

Use the local copy of the saved answers. Steps that need the network wait with a retry rather than sending people backwards.

App updated with a new step

Someone who already finished onboarding sees only the new step, then Home. Someone mid-way sees it in its place in the flow.

Answer saved, network failed

Keep the answer locally and move on. Send it to the server on the next launch or when the connection returns.

In the production app

The Equal AI app already asks its backend for onboarding status on every launch, at GET /api/v2/flows/onboarding/status. That response is the natural home for “which step is next”, so the app only needs a step-to-screen mapping plus the iOS permission check.

Applies to the V8 onboarding: phone → OTP → name and gender → pick 3 → voice → permissions → call forwarding → voicemail → welcome.
